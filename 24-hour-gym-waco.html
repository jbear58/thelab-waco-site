Who it suits

Built for people with awkward hours.

Shift workers, nurses, students, early risers, anyone whose schedule does not line up with a staffed front desk. If the reason you stopped going to a gym is that it was closed when you were free, or crowded when you got there, that is the problem this place is built around.

The Lab sits on Franklin Ave in Waco, a short drive from Woodway, Hewitt, Robinson, and Bellmead. If you want a coach in the room, the independent trainers who practice here book directly with you. If you just want a key and a quiet floor, that is the fifty-dollar membership and nothing else.

Common questions

Straight answers.

Is The Lab really open 24 hours?

Yes. Members receive keyed access and can train any hour of any day, including weekends and holidays. There is no staffed-hours restriction on floor access.

Is there a contract or a minimum commitment?

No. Membership is month to month at $50 a month plus tax, with a one-time $50 signup fee. There is no annual agreement, no minimum term, and no early-termination fee.

How much does a membership cost?

$50 per month plus tax, with a one-time $50 signup fee when you join. Optional add-ons are separate: Tools & Progress is $10 a month and Guided Training is $100 a month.

Where is The Lab located?

2912 Franklin Ave, Waco, TX 76710, a short drive from Woodway, Hewitt, Robinson, and Bellmead.

Do I have to buy personal training?

No. Coaching and massage are entirely optional and are booked directly with the independent professionals who practice at The Lab. Membership gets you the floor and nothing is bundled onto it.

Why is membership limited?

The member count is capped on purpose so the floor stays usable at peak hours. It is the reason you should not be waiting on equipment in the evening.
