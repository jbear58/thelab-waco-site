Guided Training

A real coach builds your plan around your actual body, keeps it current every month, and is in the app when you need him. You put in the work. He keeps you pointed the right way.

Coaching without the hourly

Not personal training. On purpose.

Guided Training is built and directed by a coach — but you run the training yourself, on your own schedule, on a 24/7 floor. That's why it's $100 a month instead of personal-training money: you're doing the reps, he's doing the thinking.

Before you add it

Clear direction, on your schedule.

What is included in Guided Training?

It includes a kickoff InBody scan, custom training program, personal macro targets, monthly updates, and coach support in the VirtuaGym app.

How is it different from personal training?

A coach builds and updates your plan, but you complete the training yourself. Personal training is booked directly with an independent coach for one-on-one sessions.

Is Guided Training included with membership?

No. It is a $100-per-month add-on to an active membership.
