Personal training

Independent coaches offer one-on-one coaching around your goals, schedule, and training style. Every coach at The Lab runs their own business, sets their own rate, and takes clients directly, so you are hiring the coach rather than buying a package from the gym.

Their clients hold a Lab membership, which means you have the floor 24/7 on the days you train by yourself.

What membership covers

One price, no bundle.

Membership is $50 a month plus tax, with a one-time $10 signup fee when you join. That is keyed access to the floor and the equipment, 24 hours a day, month to month, with no annual agreement. It is the only thing you have to buy.

Everything above is priced separately and can be added or dropped whenever you want. If you are just looking for a place to train at 5 a.m. or after a late shift, that is the 24 hour gym and nothing else.

Common questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to buy coaching to be a member?

No. Membership is $50 a month plus tax and stands on its own. Every service on this page is optional and priced separately.

How is Guided Training different from personal training?

Guided Training is a $100 a month add-on to membership: a custom plan, macro targets, monthly updates, and coach support in the app. Personal training is one-on-one and in person, booked directly with an independent coach who sets their own rate.

Do I need a membership to train with a coach here?

Yes. Coaches train their clients on the floor at The Lab, so their clients hold an active membership. You get 24/7 access on the days you train on your own.

What is the $10 a month add-on?

InBody body-composition scans plus the app and website progress tools. Optional for every member.
