Common questions

The fine print, up front.

Do I pay anything to train clients here?

No. No rent, no trainer fees, no commission. The gym earns its revenue from your clients' memberships, not from you.

What do my clients pay?

An active gym membership, $50 a month plus tax, which gives them 24/7 access. An optional $10 a month tools add-on covers InBody scans plus app and website tools.

What do I need to qualify?

An industry-recognized training certification or related fitness degree, and your own liability insurance. Standard requirements for an independent practice.

Am I an employee of The Lab?

No. You are an independent business using The Lab as your home base. Your methods, your pricing, and your client relationships are yours.
