Is coaching included with gym membership?

No. Membership provides 24/7 gym access. Coaching and bodywork are optional services booked directly with the independent professionals at The Lab.

Can I train on my own?

Yes. The Lab is built to be a 24/7 home base whether you train independently or add support.

What support can I add?

You can explore personal training, Guided Training, sports massage and recovery, and progress tools such as InBody scans.
